The Algebraic Square Identity

For any two real numbers $a$ and $b$, the expansion of $(a + b)²$ is calculated as $a² + 2ab + b²$. For example: $(60 + 7)² = 3600 + 840 + 49 = 4489$.

Why Do Comets Have Glowing Tails?

When a comet draws near the Sun, intense solar radiation vaporizes volatile ice from its nucleus, releasing glowing gas ions and dust into brilliant luminous tails.
