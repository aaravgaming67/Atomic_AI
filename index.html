What is a Prime Number?

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 with exactly two divisors: 1 and itself (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13).

SCIENCE

Why Does Earth Have Day and Night?

Earth rotates on its axis once every 24 hours. The side facing the Sun gets daylight, while the opposite side experiences night.

Reference: NASA Earth Science
